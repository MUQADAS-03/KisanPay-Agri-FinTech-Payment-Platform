How It Works

From mandi to bank account
in under 30 seconds.

KisanPay's RAAST Variant A flow — banked farmer, crop sale payment, Authorization → Clearing → Settlement.

1

Arthi initiates

Arthi opens KisanPay app, enters farmer CNIC + crop weight + amount. USSD *123# for feature phones.

2

RAAST authorizes

KisanPay sends ISO 20022 auth request via RAAST switch. Arthi's bank verifies balance + AML in 2–5 sec.

3

Interbank clearing

RAAST switch computes net interbank obligation. SHA-256 hash recorded in KisanPay's immutable ledger.

4

Farmer credited

SBP debits arthi's bank, credits farmer's bank. Urdu SMS confirmation. Credit score updated. Total: <30s.

About KisanPay

B2B Agri-FinTech payment orchestration platform eliminating Pakistan's PKR 459B+ annual agricultural payment inefficiency.

The Problem

PKR 459 billion
lost every year.

Pakistan's agricultural sector contributes 23% of GDP and employs 38% of the labor force, yet remains almost entirely cash-dependent. 3.2 million smallholder farmers in Punjab and Sindh are locked out of the formal financial system.

Farmers borrow at 60% APR informal rates instead of 15% formal rates — simply because they have no digital payment history. KisanPay fixes this by creating that history.
